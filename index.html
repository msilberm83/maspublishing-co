Good for It

Four people owe Eli Mercer money. He has five weeks to make good on a debt of his own.

At an aging Southern California hotel, a canceled wedding and a retirement dinner give Eli a chance to put the people who owe him to work. His sister has questions about the arrangements he’s making through her catering business. So do the people he’s trying to help.

By Arrangement

By the time a woman’s purse is stolen, her boyfriend has spent months rehearsing the rescue.

Martin Sayer arranges apparently spontaneous experiences for paying clients. Then a presidential campaign asks him to stage a public reconciliation with a grieving woman whose loss is tied to his own past.

The Front Unit

Book One of The Record

Nancy writes down the time the new tenant arrives. The record grows from there.

From her kitchen window, the homeowners association treasurer keeps track of Julian in the front unit across the courtyard. She starts with matters the board would recognize. Soon she is making corrections of her own.

I Didn’t Do Anything to Lose Except Try My Hardest

A Novel of Gambling, Family, and Memory

The balance reaches $210,000. By morning it’s gone.

Daniel goes back to the casino vacation he took at nine, armed with a recorder and the suspicion that his story starts there. His grandmother agrees to sit for the camera. She has a different account of what happened.
