Catasterism

A Novel

By Michael Silberman

ISBN 979-8-9978965-1-5
“catasterism (n.): the placing of a person among the stars.”

A man overdoses on a hotel bathroom floor, six people are shouting, and the one guest paying attention is looking at a shoe: a Gucci loafer standing upright beside the toilet, the only thing in the room that cannot send him to prison. He has spent ten years training his eyes to find things like that.

He was a lawyer once, for about ten months. Since then he has supplied the valley: a surgeon, a songwriter, a Hollywood director, a prep cook who counts his money twice. He has rules. Nobody from the route inside his house. Nobody looks back at him. Then a failing art gallery decides it is a church and asks him to write its rules. A darkly funny novel about a man who arranged his whole life so that no one would see him, and what it costs when someone finally does.

Autofiction / Memoir-Novel

I Didn't Do Anything to Lose Except Try My Hardest

A Novel of Gambling, Family, and Memory

By Michael Silberman

ISBN 979-8-9978965-0-8
“Downstairs, a quarter on the edge of an arcade cabinet holds your place. Upstairs, nobody comes looking for the adults.”

In 1995, nine-year-old Daniel spends a summer vacation in two desert casino towns with his mother, his brother, and his grandparents. While the grown-ups play the machines, the kids run the arcade on quarters and unwritten law, and a girl named Celia settles disputes at the air-hockey table until the morning she is gone.

Thirty years later, Daniel has lost his accounting career, his house, and, in one night at a high-limit machine, two hundred and ten thousand dollars that were briefly his. A counselor asks for his earliest memory of gambling. To answer, he goes looking for everyone on that casino floor. His grandmother agrees to the camera, reads every page, and corrects every memory that flatters him. A funny, unsparing novel about a family on vacation and a man who has kept score on his own life for thirty years.

The Author

Michael Silberman

Michael Silberman is a novelist based in California. His work draws upon personal experience for its emotional terrain and distinctive, conversational voice, exploring how memory reshapes the past and how people negotiate their private reckonings.

He is the author of the interconnected novels Catasterism and I Didn't Do Anything to Lose Except Try My Hardest, both published by MAS Publishing Co.

About The Imprint

MAS Publishing Co.

“We publish voice-driven fiction centered on character, consequence, and the quiet mechanics of American life.”

Founded in California, MAS Publishing Co. operates with a commitment to craft, typographical rigor, and literary works that resist easy sentimentality. From desert card rooms to Palm Springs hallways, our titles examine the architectures people build to protect themselves—and what happens when those architectures give way.
